# Limitations

1. Five local-election cycles provide limited temporal depth.
2. Historical municipality mergers and boundary changes require crosswalking and cannot be made perfectly equivalent.
3. The 2021 turnout holdout shows substantial temporal instability.
4. New parties, alliances, candidate effects and campaign events are difficult to infer from historical LGE patterns.
5. Municipality-level demographic relationships are ecological associations, not individual voter behaviour.
6. Census values between 2011 and 2022 are interpolated for descriptive alignment; this introduces modelling assumptions.
7. Current demographic context is not used to contaminate historical holdouts.
8. Party categories have unequal predictability.
9. Historical-MAE sensitivity bands are not confidence or prediction intervals.
10. 2026 outputs are model-based scenarios rather than known future results.
